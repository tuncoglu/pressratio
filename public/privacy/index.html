Privacy policy

Pasta & Noodle Calculator (Android app and website at pasta.sourdoughcalculator.uk). Last updated 31 October 2026.

The short version

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device. The only time anything leaves it is when you ask for the local temperature, and then only rounded coordinates are sent.

What is stored on your device

Your calculator settings, the batch log you keep, and your calibration results are saved in your browser's local storage on your device (the Android app is a wrapper around the same website and uses the same storage). We have no copy of it and cannot see it. You can export it as a JSON file, import it back, or delete it by clearing the app's storage in Android settings.

Location (optional)

The calculator can look up the outdoor temperature to adjust the dough rest time. This only happens if you tap “Use my location”, or if you have ticked “Detect automatically when I open the app”. Your device then asks your permission to share an approximate location.

  • Your latitude and longitude are rounded to about 1 km on your device, then sent to Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) to get the current temperature. Open-Meteo is a free weather service; see its own privacy policy for how it handles requests.
  • Nothing else is sent: no identifier, no account, no device details we add. Like any web request, Open-Meteo receives your IP address.
  • The location is not stored by us, and the app keeps only the temperature that came back, not your coordinates.
  • If you decline, or you are offline, you simply type the temperature yourself. Nothing else in the app needs location.

Our servers

The website is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address and request details to deliver the pages and may keep standard server logs. We do not run analytics or set cookies, and we do not combine any of this with other data. Once the app has loaded, it works offline.

Children

The app is a kitchen calculator and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising. The one third party involved is Open-Meteo, only as described above and only when you ask for the temperature.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
