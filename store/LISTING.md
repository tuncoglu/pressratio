## Full description (4000 chars max)

Pasta & Noodle Calculator works out how much flour, egg and water your dough needs, for the machine you actually use. Type the egg you have and it tells you the flour to weigh, with a range rather than a false promise: it is guidance, not guarantee, and the squeeze test has the final say.

### Three kinds of dough
- Extruded semolina and egg for manual presses (Bigolaro-style) and electric extruders. Dies are listed per model, and thin dies get the wetter half of the range.
- Rolled egg pasta for ribbons, ravioli and lasagne, by rolling pin or pasta roller, with a knead test and the sheet size your dough makes.
- Asian alkaline noodles, scaled from the egg, with baker's percentages and the baking-soda weight before baking.

### Built around how you really measure
- Weigh the egg shelled, weigh it in the shell, or just count eggs: the app shows how much error that adds to the flour figure.
- Bigolaro loads: see how many cylinder loads your dough fills, and scale the recipe to whole loads.
- Blend flours (00, semolina, bread flour) and see the blend's protein.
- Ravioli filling guide: how many ravioli your dough makes, filling needed, and servings.
- Rest time that adjusts to your kitchen temperature.

### Learns your own dough
- Run the squeeze test, log the water or semolina you add, and rate each batch.
- After a few good batches the calculator offers your own median hydration as the target.
- Your batch log can be exported and imported as a file.

### Honest about what it knows
Numbers that are still placeholders are labelled "uncalibrated" wherever they affect a result. Sources are cited in the app and the project's science review.

### Private by design
No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your recipes and batch log stay on your phone and the app works offline after the first load. The only network request is optional: if you tap "Use my location", your coordinates, rounded to about 1 km, go to Open-Meteo to read the outdoor temperature. Prefer not to share it? Type the temperature yourself.

The same calculator runs in any browser at pasta.sourdoughcalculator.uk.
